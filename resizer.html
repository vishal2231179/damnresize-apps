How to resize an image online

  1. Click "Select Image File" and choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter the new width or height in pixels. Keep "Lock aspect ratio" selected to avoid stretching the image.
  3. Click "Apply Dimensions" and check the preview.
  4. Click "Download Resized Image" to save the file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no signup and no watermark.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. This page resizes the image in your browser using the canvas API, so your file stays on your device.

Which image formats can I resize?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images. The resized image is downloaded as a JPEG file.

Why does my image look stretched after resizing?

This happens when the new width and height do not match the original proportions. Keep "Lock aspect ratio" selected, or crop the image first.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Making an image smaller usually keeps it sharp, but enlarging it can make it look soft or blurry. Preview the result before you download it.
