Free online image and PDF tools for forms, exams and everyday work

DamnResize.in brings together simple tools to resize, compress, crop and convert images, turn files into PDF, and prepare photos and signatures for online applications. There is no signup, and every tool is free to use. Many tools process your file directly in your browser, but processing can differ by tool, so please review our Privacy Policy for details.

What you can do with DamnResize.in

  • Resize images to exact pixel dimensions or by percentage.
  • Compress images to reduce file size in KB for forms and uploads.
  • Crop images to remove unwanted edges.
  • Convert image formats such as JPG, PNG and WebP.
  • Prepare exam photos and signatures for government and job applications.
  • Convert JPG to PDF, and use other PDF conversion tools.
  • Scan documents to PDF using your device camera or images.

How to resize a photo for an online form

  1. Read the application notice and note the required dimensions, file format and maximum file size.
  2. Upload your photo or signature to the tool.
  3. Enter the required width and height, or choose a preset as a starting point.
  4. Adjust the compression level until the file size is within the allowed limit.
  5. Preview the result, download the file, and check it against the notice before uploading.

For a detailed checklist, read our SSC photo and signature upload guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is DamnResize.in free to use?

Yes. The tools are free, with no signup required.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Many tools work in your browser, but processing varies by tool. Please check the Privacy Policy for how files are handled.

Can I resize a photo to a specific size in KB?

Yes. Resize the image to the required dimensions first, then lower the compression quality until the output size shown is within the limit.

Will a resized photo always be accepted by the exam portal?

Not necessarily. Requirements can change, so always compare the final file with the dimensions, format and size limits in the current official notice.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload common formats such as JPG, PNG and WebP. Some tools download the result as a JPEG file.
