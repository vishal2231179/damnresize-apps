Privacy Policy & Security

We respect your absolute privacy. Your files never leave your device.

100% Client-Side Processing

At DamnResize.in, all image resizing, compression, cropping, and conversion tasks are performed entirely inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side JavaScript.

This means when you upload a photo or signature, **it is never uploaded to our servers or stored anywhere in the cloud**. Your private documents, identity photos, and personal images remain completely secure on your own device.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use standard analytics tools or third-party advertising partners (like Google AdSense) to serve ads and analyze site performance. These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website.

If you have any questions regarding our privacy policy or website security, feel free to reach out to us through our official channels.
