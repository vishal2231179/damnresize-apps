Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use DamnResize.in.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Files and tool processing

Processing depends on the tool you choose. Some image tools process files in your browser; other tools may rely on additional libraries or services. Check the information shown on the individual tool page before using it with sensitive files.

Do not assume that every file on every tool is processed only on your device. We do not make a site-wide promise that files are never transmitted or stored unless the specific tool clearly states and technically supports that behavior.

Website and third-party services

Our hosting provider may process technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, request details, and basic diagnostic logs, according to its policies and settings.

The site may load third-party scripts, styles, or other resources. Those providers may receive standard connection data from your browser when the resource is requested. Their privacy policies govern their processing.

Contact form

If you submit the contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic, and message you provide so we can respond. The form is processed by a Cloudflare Worker, uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated spam, and is sent through Resend to our support email address. The email address you provide is used as the reply-to address; please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive identity documents.

Cloudflare, Resend, and the mailbox provider may process or retain information as needed to deliver, secure, and store the message under their respective policies. Contact us at support@damnresize.in to ask about a submitted message.

Cookies and advertising

If analytics or advertising services, including Google AdSense, are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to operate, measure, or personalize their services, subject to your settings and applicable requirements. Review the provider's privacy information and your browser controls for details.

We will update this policy if the services used on the site change. The exact cookies and choices available depend on which services are enabled and your location.

Your choices

Avoid uploading or processing information you do not have permission to use. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking some resources may affect how parts of the site work.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, email support@damnresize.in.
