How to prepare your exam photo and signature

Online application forms for SSC, banking, UPSC and state police recruitment usually ask for a passport-size photo and a signature with a fixed format, dimensions and file size. This tool helps you resize and compress both files in your browser so they are closer to those requirements. The processing happens on your device, and this page does not send your files to a server.

Steps

  1. Select your target examination from the list.
  2. Upload your photo and your signature in the two boxes.
  3. Click "Process & Download" for each file.
  4. Check the dimensions and file size of the downloaded image against the current official notice.
  5. Upload the files to the application form.

Tips for a clean photo and signature

  • Use a recent, well-lit photo with a plain background and a clearly visible face.
  • Sign with a dark pen on plain white paper, and photograph it in good light.
  • Keep the signature fully inside the frame so nothing is cut off.
  • Do not rely on a preset alone. Recruitment notices change, so always confirm the latest requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo and signature size should I use for my exam?

The correct size is stated in the official notice or application form for your exam and year. The presets here are starting points, so compare the downloaded file with the notice before you upload.

Are my photo and signature uploaded to a server?

No. This page resizes and compresses images in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Why was my photo or signature rejected on the portal?

Common reasons include a wrong file size, wrong dimensions, an unsupported format, a blurry image, or a signature that is cut off. Compare your file with the notice and try again.

Which file formats can I upload here?

You can upload JPG, JPEG or PNG images. The processed file is downloaded as a JPEG.

Is this tool free?

Yes. It is free to use and does not require signup.
