Why use DamnResize Image Compressor?

Online forms, job portals, and websites often enforce image file-size limits. This free image compressor reduces JPG, PNG, and WebP file size directly in your browser. Your image is not uploaded to any server, so your files stay private. Reducing file size can lower image quality, so always preview the result before downloading.

How to compress an image online

  1. Click "Select Image" and choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP file.
  2. Move the Compression Level slider to reduce the file size.
  3. Check the Compressed Size shown next to the original size.
  4. Preview the image to make sure the quality is acceptable.
  5. Click "Download Compressed Image" to save the JPEG file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens in your browser using the canvas API, so your image stays on your device.

How do I compress an image to under 100 KB?

Upload the image and lower the Compression Level until the Compressed Size shows below 100 KB. If the quality drops too much, resize the image dimensions first using our Image Resizer tool.

Which formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images. The compressed output is downloaded as a JPEG file.

Will compression reduce image quality?

Yes, lower compression levels reduce quality. Use the preview to find the best balance between file size and clarity.
